🔍 What Is jev-style?

jev-style is a smart decision-making tool that runs entirely on your own computer. It uses small, carefully tuned "decision models" to help you analyze choices, route questions, and guard against errors—all without sending your data anywhere. Think of it as a smart assistant that lives on your machine, works offline, and gives you fast, calibrated answers.

This application brings six ready-to-use agent skills to your fingertips. Whether you're sorting through information, checking reasoning, or setting up safety guardrails, jev-style gives you the power of advanced AI models without the need for expensive cloud services or programming experience.
